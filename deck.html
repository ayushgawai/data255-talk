Problem statement

Drive safely, on the car's own computer, in every situation

Perceive with cameras, lidar and radar → predict every road user's trajectory → choose a safe path, many times per second, inside a restricted operating domain.

01 · LATENCY

Decisions run on the vehicle. The largest models cannot sit in the control loop.

02 · THE LONG TAIL

Most risk lives in events too rare to collect: a car on fire, a flooded street, a child stepping out behind an SUV.

03 · VERIFIABILITY

Regulators, insurers and riders need statistical evidence of safety, not a convincing demo.

Problem and motivation

Labels don't scale. Can vision learn like BERT?

Language · BERT hides 15%
Thecarstoppedatthe[MASK] → redlight

A word is dense in meaning. Guessing it needs understanding.

Vision · a patch is redundant
15% hidden: neighbours give it away
75% hidden: must understand the scene
1 · Architecture gap

CNNs slide over grids, so mask tokens and positions did not fit. Vision Transformers removed this.

2 · Information density

Pixels are redundant, so masking must be very aggressive to stop simple interpolation.

3 · Role of the decoder

Vision decoders predict low-level pixels, so decoder design sets how semantic the features become.

Method

An asymmetric encoder and decoder

Loss: mean squared error in pixel space, only on masked patches.
Cheap decoder: about 9% of the encoder's compute per token.
After pretraining: discard the decoder, keep and fine-tune the encoder.
# random masking, as in the official models_mae.py noise = torch.rand(N, L) # one score per patch ids_shuffle = torch.argsort(noise, dim=1) ids_restore = torch.argsort(ids_shuffle, dim=1) ids_keep = ids_shuffle[:, :int(L * 0.25)] # keep 25% x_vis = torch.gather(x, 1, ids_keep[..., None].expand(-1, -1, D))

Critique from four perspectives

Reviewer, patent board, code review, authors

📝

(a) Peer reviewer

Strong accept
  • Simple idea, clearly explained by information density
  • Thorough ablations; wall-clock numbers, not only FLOPs
  • Incremental over context encoders, iGPT and BEiT; linear probing lags
  • Almost all evidence is ViT on ImageNet
🏛️

(b) Industry and patent board

Fund use · no patent
  • Removes the labelling bottleneck: medical, inspection, satellite
  • About 3× cheaper pretraining for an in-house backbone
  • Dense prior art; a narrow claim is hard to detect and enforce
  • Released weights are CC BY-NC 4.0: retrain for commercial use
💻

(c) Code review · facebookresearch/mae

  • One readable file, models_mae.py; batched argsort masking
  • Reuses timm ViT blocks and the DeiT recipe; public checkpoints
  • Pinned to timm 0.3.2; no unit tests; archived August 2025
  • PyTorch re-implementation, not the TPU code behind the paper
👥

(d) Authors and motivation

  • He: ResNet, Mask R-CNN · Chen: MoCo v3, SimSiam · Xie: ResNeXt
  • Li: ViTDet · Dollár: COCO, FPN · Girshick: the R-CNN family
  • The team that built the contrastive baselines and detection benchmarks replaced its own baselines, then tested on its own benchmarks
